## Reflection: Agile Team Charter

Creating the Agile Team Charter helped our team understand the importance of having clear goals, roles and responsibilities. Each team member was assigned a role based on the work they were expected to contribute to the SafeStreet Community Security Solutions project. This helped us understand our individual responsibilities and how we could work together to achieve the project goals.

I also learned that communication, teamwork and respect are important when working on an Agile project. Our communication plan helped us decide how we would share information and discuss challenges. The milestones also helped us organise the project into smaller stages so that we could monitor our progress and work towards meeting deadlines.

One challenge is making sure that all team members communicate regularly and complete their tasks on time. Overall, the Agile Team Charter gave our team a clear direction and helped us understand how to work together more effectively.
