# Agile Logbook Reflection

## Overview
The Agile Logbook was used to record the progress and activities completed during the SafeStreet Community Security Solutions project. It documented the work carried out during different stages of the project, including designing the application, preparing presentations, collecting feedback, creating the Trello Sprint Board, and working on the Business Model and Solution Architecture.

## Activities and Progress
The logbook helped us keep track of our project activities and the progress made over time. We recorded the tasks completed, including creating Figma wireframes, presenting our ideas, reviewing feedback, organising work on Trello, and planning the proposed solution. These activities helped us move from the initial idea towards a more organised project.

## Team Collaboration
Working on SafeStreet required communication and cooperation between team members. The logbook helped document our progress and made it easier to reflect on the work completed. Team collaboration was important because different members contributed to research, writing, design, and other project activities.

## What I Learned
I learned that an Agile Logbook is useful for tracking progress and reflecting on the development process. It also helps identify completed activities, challenges, and areas that need improvement. Recording project activities provides evidence of the work completed and helps the team remain organised.

## Challenges and Improvements
One challenge was keeping track of the different activities and making sure the project work remained organised. In future projects, I would update the logbook consistently after each major activity, record challenges in more detail, and include clear evidence of completed tasks. This would make it easier to monitor progress and identify areas that need attention.

## Conclusion
The Agile Logbook helped document the progress of the SafeStreet project and reflect on the team's activities and learning experiences. It showed the importance of recording progress, communicating with team members, and reviewing work throughout an Agile project.
