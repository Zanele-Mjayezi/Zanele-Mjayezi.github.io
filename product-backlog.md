# Product Backlog Reflection

## Overview
The Product Backlog was created for the SafeStreet Community Security Solutions project using Trello. It contained approximately 50 cards representing the different user stories and tasks required to develop the application. The backlog helped the team organise the project requirements and keep track of the work that needed to be completed.

## How We Used the Product Backlog
The team used Trello to organise user stories and tasks related to the SafeStreet application. These included researching community security problems, interviewing Khayelitsha residents and traders, analysing feedback, and planning application features. Each card represented a piece of work that helped the team understand and develop the proposed solution.

## What I Learned
Creating and managing the Product Backlog helped me understand the importance of organising project requirements before and during development. I learned that breaking a large project into smaller user stories and tasks makes the work easier to understand, assign, and manage. Trello also helped the team keep project activities organised in one place.

## Challenges and Improvements
One challenge was managing a large number of cards and ensuring that the tasks were clear to team members. In future projects, I would improve the backlog by reviewing priorities regularly, removing duplicate items, and making sure that every user story has clear acceptance criteria. This would help the team focus on the most important requirements first.

## Conclusion
The Product Backlog supported the planning and organisation of the SafeStreet project. It provided an overview of the work required and helped the team manage project requirements using an Agile approach.
